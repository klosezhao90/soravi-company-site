帮助与支持 Help & Support

内容版本 / Content version: 2026-10-16

请邮件联系 contact@kzsoravi.com，说明 App 版本、设备型号、系统版本及复现步骤。截图可选，请先遮挡私人信息。

Email contact@kzsoravi.com with the app version, device model, system version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove private information first.

免费内容与购买 / Free content and purchases

当前首发阅读内容免费开放，没有订阅或广告。新购暂未开放。已有购买权益、恢复购买及原有永久包含当前与后续全部更新的承诺继续保留。

Current launch reading content is free, with no subscription or advertising. New purchases are not currently offered. Existing purchases, restoration, and the original promise of permanent access to all current and future updates remain honored.

恢复购买 / Restore purchases

使用原购买所用的 Apple 账户，在设置页选择“恢复购买”。这可能需要网络。恢复权益不会恢复收藏；账单与退款由 Apple 处理。请勿向支持人员提供 Apple 账户密码。

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase and choose Restore Purchases in Settings. A connection may be required. Restoring access does not restore favorites. Apple handles billing and refunds; never send your Apple Account password to support.

拼音与英译 / Pinyin and translations

11 首精选已完成 AI 内容校核，包含原文对照、拼音、英译、字词和赏析；这不等同于人工文学审定。参考诗库英译已做全量数据检查并修正确认的错配，仍未逐首进行文学审定。自动拼音中的未解析读音不作猜写；底本缺文或损坏会明确提示。若发现问题，请附诗名、作者和原句。

All eleven curated readings have received an AI content check covering source text, pinyin, translation, glosses, and commentary. This is distinct from human literary approval. Corpus translations have undergone exhaustive data checks and confirmed mapping errors have been repaired, but not every translation has received a literary review. Unresolved readings are not guessed; missing or damaged source text is identified. Report issues with the title, author, and source line.

搜索与浏览 / Search and browsing

诗库可搜索中文作者、标题、正文及已有英译；精选精读另外支持英文作者和无声调拼音。结果按页显示，底部“加载更多”可继续查看。按朝代浏览作者时，仅显示该朝代下收录的作品。

Search the corpus by Chinese author, title, source text, or available English translation. Curated readings also support English author names and unaccented pinyin. Use Load More at the bottom for additional matches. Browsing an author within a dynasty shows only works catalogued under that dynasty.

收藏、每日诗与朗读 / Favorites, daily poems, and recitation

收藏保存在本机，应用未提供跨设备收藏同步。每日诗从当前 11 首精选中按本地日期选择，会循环出现；小组件更新由系统调度。朗读目前用于精选原文，若没有可用中文语音，请检查系统语音设置。

Favorites stay on this device; there is no app-provided cross-device favorites sync. The daily poem cycles through the current 11 curated poems by local date, and the system schedules widget refreshes. Recitation is available for curated source text; check system voice settings if a Chinese voice is unavailable.
