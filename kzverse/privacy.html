隐私政策 Privacy Policy

内容版本 / Content version: 2026-10-16

KZ Verse 由云南索拉维数字科技有限公司运营。本政策说明设备本地数据、Apple 服务，以及你主动发给我们的支持邮件如何处理。

KZ Verse is operated by Yunnan Soravi Digital Technology Co., Ltd. This policy explains local app data, Apple services, and support messages you choose to send us.

设备上的信息 / On-device information

收藏和购买权益状态保存在设备本地，包括应用的 App Group 容器。App Group 是本地共享容器，不是 iCloud 同步服务。小组件按本地日期从内置诗库选诗。本应用无需注册账号，也不向我们运营的服务器上传你的收藏或阅读记录。

Favorites and purchase-access status are stored locally, including in the app’s App Group container. An App Group is local storage, not an iCloud sync service. Widgets select a poem from bundled content using the local date. No KZ Verse account is required, and the app does not upload your favorites or reading history to our servers.

离线阅读与 Apple 服务 / Offline reading and Apple services

内置诗词、拼音、已有英译和每日诗可离线阅读。朗读使用 Apple 系统语音，可用语音与设备设置有关。Apple 处理购买、付款、退款和恢复购买；应用通过 StoreKit 在设备上确认权益，我们不接收银行卡信息。恢复购买、下载系统语音或其他 Apple 服务可能需要网络，并受 Apple 的隐私规则约束。

Bundled poems, pinyin, available translations, and the daily poem can be read offline. Recitation uses Apple’s system voices, whose availability depends on the device. Apple handles purchases, payment, refunds, and restoration. StoreKit verifies access on the device; we do not receive card details. Restoration, voice downloads, and other Apple services may require a connection and follow Apple’s privacy practices.

广告与分析 / Advertising and analytics

应用没有第三方广告或分析 SDK，不跟踪你在其他公司的应用或网站中的活动。Apple 可能按其服务规则和你的设备设置处理 App Store、购买或诊断信息。

The app includes no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Apple may process App Store, purchase, or diagnostic information according to its services and your device settings.

联系支持 / Contacting support

你主动发送邮件至 contact@kzsoravi.com 时，我们及邮件服务商会收到你的邮箱、正文和自愿附上的附件，仅用于处理请求、跟进问题及履行适用义务。请勿发送密码或银行卡资料。打开外部网站或邮件应用会涉及相应服务商的网络与隐私规则。

If you email contact@kzsoravi.com, we and our email provider receive your address, message, and attachments you choose to include. We use them to handle your request, follow up, and meet applicable obligations. Do not send passwords or payment card details. External websites and email apps follow their own network and privacy practices.

保留与删除 / Retention and deletion

在诗页取消收藏即可删除相应本地收藏。其余本地信息保留到应用或操作系统清除对应数据为止。卸载应用不会删除 Apple 的购买记录、其他设备的数据或已有系统备份；设备备份与迁移由你的 Apple 设置控制，我们没有可用于恢复收藏的服务器副本。支持邮件按处理请求及适用义务所需时间保留；你可邮件请求访问、更正或删除主动发送的信息，法定保留要求可能限制删除。

Unfavorite a poem to remove its local favorite record. Other local information remains until the app or operating system removes it. Uninstalling does not delete Apple purchase records, another device’s data, or existing system backups. Apple backup and migration depend on your settings; we have no server copy from which to recover favorites. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. Email us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent; legal retention requirements may limit deletion.

问题与更新 / Questions and updates

隐私问题请联系 contact@kzsoravi.com。处理方式改变时，我们会更新本文及版本日期。

For privacy questions, email contact@kzsoravi.com. We will update this policy and its version date if our practices change.
