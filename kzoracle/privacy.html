Privacy Policy

Content version: 2026-10-16

KZ Oracle is operated by 云南索拉维数字科技有限公司 (Yunnan Soravi Digital Technology Co., Ltd.). This policy explains how the app handles information on your devices, Apple services, and information you choose to send us.

Information kept on your device

KZ Oracle saves a randomly generated draw seed, the last day you drew a lot, your draw history, and any ‘resonated’ marks. The seed keeps your daily draw consistent; it is not an advertising identifier. If you create a Bazi profile, the app also saves your selected birth date, birth time if supplied, and whether the time is known. The app calculates your chart on the device and caches whether your purchase unlock is available. No KZ Oracle account is required.

Widgets and Apple Watch

The iPhone or iPad app uses an Apple App Group container to store information locally and share it with its widgets. With a paired Apple Watch, the iPhone app sends the draw seed and last draw day through Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. The Watch keeps a local copy so it can show the daily lot. This Watch sync does not send your birth information or full draw history. KZ Oracle does not provide its own cloud account or cloud history-sync service.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple processes purchases, payment details, refunds, and restoration through the App Store. KZ Oracle uses StoreKit transaction information on the device to determine whether Full Unlock is available. We do not receive your payment card details. Purchases, restoration, and other Apple system services may require a network connection and are subject to Apple’s privacy practices. The bundled verses, daily draw, and calendar calculations can be used without a connection.

Sharing and contacting support

A lot card leaves the app only when you choose a destination in the system share sheet. The generated card contains the selected lot’s content, not your birth profile or draw seed; the receiving app or service handles what you share under its own policies. If you email contact@kzsoravi.com, we and our email provider receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. We use this information to answer your request and resolve the reported issue. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or birth information that is unnecessary for your request. Opening an external website or email app may involve that provider’s network and privacy practices.

Analytics and advertising

KZ Oracle includes no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. The app does not upload your birth profile, draw seed, history, or marks to a server operated by us. Apple may process App Store, purchase, or diagnostic information according to its services and your device settings.

Keeping and removing information

Local information remains until you remove it or the operating system removes the app’s stored data. In Today, open About & Support, then choose Reset birth info to remove the saved birth date and time from this device. This does not require Full Unlock and does not erase draw history or purchases. Removing the app from a device does not erase Apple’s purchase records, copies on another device, previously shared cards, or existing system backups. Depending on your settings, Apple device backups may contain app data and may restore it later; manage those backups in Apple’s settings. We do not hold a server copy of your local history to recover or erase. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request, follow up on the issue, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can email us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent to support; legal retention requirements may limit deletion.

Questions and policy changes

For privacy questions, contact contact@kzsoravi.com. If our practices change, we will update this policy and its version date.
