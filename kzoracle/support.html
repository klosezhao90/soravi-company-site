Help & Support

Content version: 2026-10-16

For help with KZ Oracle, email contact@kzsoravi.com. Include the app version, your device model and system version, what happened, and the steps that led to it. Screenshots are optional; remove private information before sending them.

What is free, and what does Full Unlock include?

Your daily draw and the complete reading for today’s lot are free. Full Unlock is a one-time purchase for your Bazi chart and the ability to revisit drawn lots in the Album. All future lots, features, and updates are included. There is no subscription and no advertising. The purchase screen shows the price for your App Store region before you buy.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase. In Today, open About & Support and choose Restore Purchases; the same action is available on the Full Unlock purchase screen. An internet connection may be needed. If access is still missing, check your App Store purchase history and contact us with the error message. Do not send your Apple Account password or payment card details. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Why can I draw only once a day?

Each device installation has a saved draw seed. Together with the local calendar day, it gives a consistent lot for that day. Return on the next local day for a new draw. The same lot can appear on different days, so collecting all 100 lots is not guaranteed in 100 days. Keep your device date and time correct.

Why does my widget or Watch show an older state?

Widgets depend on the system’s refresh schedule. Open KZ Oracle to check today’s current state and allow the widget time to refresh. The Watch companion displays a lot drawn on its paired iPhone; it does not draw independently. Open the app on both devices and check that the Watch is paired and connected. Watch synchronization may be delayed while devices are disconnected.

How do I remove my birth information?

In Today, open About & Support, choose Reset birth info, and confirm. This removes the saved birth date and time from this device without requiring Full Unlock. It leaves your draw history and purchase unchanged. The unlocked Chart also has Reset birth info. System backups and information on other devices are managed separately; see the Privacy Policy.

Will my Album appear on another iPhone or iPad?

KZ Oracle has no account-based cloud sync for your Album or birth profile. Restoring a purchase restores eligible access, not your saved draw history. Apple’s device backup and migration tools may transfer app data according to your settings, but we cannot recover local history from a server. The paired Watch receives only the draw seed and last draw day.

What is the app’s guidance for?

The lot verses, traditional almanac, and Bazi chart are cultural references for personal reflection and enjoyment. They do not guarantee future outcomes and should not replace your own judgment or professional medical, legal, or financial advice.
