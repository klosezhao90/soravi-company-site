帮助与支持 / Help & Support

For help, email contact@kzsoravi.com with the app version, device model, system version, what happened, and steps to reproduce it. Screenshots are optional: hide health information before sending.

The daily recovery score and four-signal summary remain free, always. There is no subscription or advertising. The purchase screen shows the actual price for your App Store region before you buy.

连接与缺少数据 / Connection and missing data

在今日页选择 Connect Apple Health 并自行选择允许读取的类型。保持兼容的 Apple Watch 或其他数据来源完成记录与同步。每个指标至少需要 3 个有效基线日，最多使用前 7 条每日记录。今天没有数据、尚无基线或未允许读取时，App 不生成虚假分数。HealthKit 不会向 App 区分拒绝读取与没有数据；检查权限、同步后下拉刷新。

Choose Connect Apple Health on Today and select the reading permissions yourself. Allow a compatible Apple Watch or another supported data source to record and sync. Each signal needs at least 3 valid baseline days, using up to 7 preceding daily records. Missing readings, an unfinished baseline, or unavailable access do not produce an invented score. HealthKit does not distinguish denied reading access from absent data to the app; check permissions and sync, then pull down to refresh.

如何解读 / Understanding comparisons

恢复分反映评分模型相对你个人近期基线的变化，不是人群正常范围、疾病判断或训练处方。HRV 使用 SDNN，评分采用对数尺度；深度页的毫秒值与百分比差值使用原始值。缺失指标不按零计算，剩余权重重新分配。睡眠区间重叠仅计一次，间隔不超过两小时的片段归组到结束日，清醒间隔不算睡眠。

The score describes this model’s change relative to your own recent baseline, not a population reference range, diagnosis, or training prescription. HRV is SDNN and uses a logarithmic scale for scoring; the deep-reading milliseconds and percentage difference use raw values. Missing signals are not zero; remaining weights are redistributed. Overlapping sleep intervals count once; episodes separated by at most two hours are grouped under the ending day, excluding awake gaps.

恢复购买 / Restoring purchases

使用原购买时的 Apple 账户，在 About 或付费页选择 Restore Purchases；可能需要网络连接。若仍未恢复，检查 App Store 购买记录并联系我们提供错误提示，不发送密码或付款资料。Apple 处理账单和退款。TestFlight 中的购买使用 Apple 沙盒，不代表正式商店购买验收。

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase, then choose Restore Purchases in About or on the purchase screen; a network connection may be needed. If access is still missing, check App Store purchase history and contact us with the error message, without passwords or payment details. Apple handles billing and refunds. TestFlight purchases use Apple’s sandbox and do not establish production purchase validation.

小组件刷新 / Widget updates

先打开主 App 同步最新健康读数，再查看小组件。小组件显示最近一次有效快照，跨日或无有效分数时显示引导；系统控制实际刷新时机。你可以移除主屏幕或锁屏小组件以避免旁人看到恢复分。

Open the main app to refresh health readings, then check the widget. The widget shows the last valid snapshot and guidance when the score is absent or from another day; the system controls actual refresh timing. Remove Home or Lock Screen widgets if you do not want others to see your score.

健康用途边界 / Wellness limitations

KZ Recovery 仅提供一般健康与训练参考，不是医疗器械，不诊断、治疗、治愈或预防疾病。数据来源、采样密度、短期基线与当前不完整的一天都会影响结果。有健康疑问请咨询医生，紧急情况联系当地急救服务。

KZ Recovery offers wellness and training insights only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Recording sources, sparse samples, a short baseline, and an incomplete current day can affect results. Consult a doctor about health concerns and contact local emergency services in an emergency.
