隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

内容版本 / Content version: 2026-10-23

KZ Recovery 由云南索拉维数字科技有限公司运营。本政策说明本地健康信息、Apple 服务和你主动发送给支持团队的信息如何处理。

KZ Recovery is operated by Yunnan Soravi Digital Technology Co., Ltd. This policy explains local health information, Apple services, and information you choose to send to support.

健康数据 / Health data

在你允许后，App 只读 Apple 健康中的心率变异性（SDNN）、静息心率、呼吸频率和睡眠。App 在设备内按本地日期聚合数据，计算相对个人基线的恢复分和趋势；不向健康数据库写入记录。我们不会将这些健康数据上传到自有服务器、出售、用于广告或用户画像。

With your permission, the app reads heart rate variability (SDNN), resting heart rate, respiratory rate, and sleep from Apple Health. It aggregates local daily records and calculates a score and trends relative to your own baseline on this device. It does not write records to Apple Health. We do not upload these health data to our servers, sell them, or use them for advertising or profiling.

本地信息与小组件 / Local information and widgets

健康读数和历史数据在使用期间保留在 App 内存中。App 在本地 App Group 中保存最近一次恢复分、日期、分档、更新时间和购买解锁状态，供主 App 与小组件使用；小组件不直接读取 HealthKit。恢复分属于健康相关信息，添加到主屏幕或锁屏前请考虑旁人可见性。App 不提供自建云同步。

Health readings and history are held in app memory while in use. A local App Group stores the latest score, date, readiness band, update time, and purchase-unlock state for the app and its widget; the widget does not query HealthKit directly. A recovery score is health-related information: consider who can see your Home or Lock Screen before adding a widget. The app provides no developer-operated cloud sync.

Apple 服务与购买 / Apple services and purchases

Apple 通过 App Store 处理购买、付款、退款和恢复。App 在设备上读取 StoreKit 交易信息以核对权益；我们不接收银行卡资料。购买和恢复可能需要网络连接，适用 Apple 的隐私规则。Apple 也可能根据你的设备设置处理诊断数据、设备备份和健康数据同步；这不属于我们运营的服务器。

Apple handles purchases, payment, refunds, and restoration through the App Store. The app checks StoreKit transactions on the device to verify access; we do not receive payment card details. Purchases and restoration may need a network connection and are subject to Apple’s privacy practices. Apple may also process diagnostics, device backups, and Health data sync according to your settings; these are not servers operated by us.

广告、分析与支持 / Advertising, analytics and support

App 无账号、无广告和第三方分析 SDK，不跨 App 或网站追踪你。你主动发送邮件时，我们和邮件服务商会接收邮箱地址、正文及附件，用于答复和处理问题。请勿发送密码、付款资料或不必要的健康读数。打开外部网页或邮件软件时，适用对应服务商的隐私规则。

The app has no account, advertising, or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. If you email support, we and our email provider receive your address, message, and attachments to answer and resolve the request. Do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary health readings. External websites and email apps have their own privacy practices.

控制、保留与删除 / Controls, retention and removal

你可以在 Apple 健康或系统设置中管理本 App 的读取权限。权限被拒绝与数据不存在时，HealthKit 不向 App 区分原因。移除小组件可停止在该位置展示；删除 App 可移除系统管理的本地 App 数据，但不会删除 Apple 健康记录、购买记录、其他设备副本或现有备份。已有备份可能按系统设置恢复数据。支持邮件仅按处理请求、后续跟进及适用法律义务所需保留；可联系我们请求访问、更正或删除主动发送的信息。

Manage the app’s reading permissions in Apple Health or system Settings. HealthKit does not tell the app whether a missing reading reflects denied access or absent data. Remove a widget to stop showing the score there. Deleting the app removes its system-managed local app data, but does not erase Apple Health records, purchase records, other-device copies, or existing backups. System backups may restore app data later. Support correspondence is retained as needed for your request, follow-up, and applicable legal obligations; contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent.

政策更新与联系 / Policy updates and contact

如处理方式改变，我们会更新政策和版本日期。隐私问题请联系 contact@kzsoravi.com。

If our practices change, we will update this policy and its version date. For privacy questions, contact contact@kzsoravi.com.
